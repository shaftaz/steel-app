# LinkedIn draft — The queue (cbam-verification-bottleneck)

The CBAM number nobody's watching: 12,000 declarant applications. 403 accredited verifiers. Three accreditation bodies open to third-country applicants.

First declarations: 30 September 2027. On-site audits: mandatory. Verification: €5k–50k per facility — cheap against default-value charges that scale toward €463–720/tonne for high-intensity stainless by 2034.

In a queue economy, the cheapest thing you can buy is your place in line. Exporters who book verifiers late won't be priced at defaults because their steel is dirty — but because nobody was available to check.

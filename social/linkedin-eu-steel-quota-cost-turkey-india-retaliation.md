# LinkedIn draft — The invoice (eu-steel-quota-cost-turkey-india-retaliation)

The EU steel wall now has an itemized invoice.

Turkey: ~$3bn/year in lost exports — 1.2 Mt of HR sheet, 369kt rebar, 263kt wire rod. India: quotas exhausted, cargoes stranded at EU ports, allocations roughly halved — and a proposed WTO retaliation citing $6.92bn in losses since 2018.

The escalation is the story: defensive measures redistribute pain within steel; retaliation exports the dispute to other sectors entirely. And multi-million tonnes of displaced steel must land somewhere — the Gulf, Africa, and SEA should expect the surge, and on current form, respond with walls of their own.

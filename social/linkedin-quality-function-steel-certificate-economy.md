# LinkedIn draft — Certificate economy (quality-function-steel-certificate-economy)

Steel used to sell on chemistry and price. Increasingly, it sells on certificates.

BIS licences gate India. PSL 2 evidence gates pipeline tenders. API monograms gate the new CCUS pipe market. CBAM verification gates Europe — at punitive default rates for anyone who can't prove their emissions.

Every market now asks the same question in a different language: prove it. Which means the quality function just stopped being a cost centre — it's the gatekeeper of revenue. The CQI has argued for years that quality belongs in the boardroom. Steel's regulators just made the argument unanswerable.

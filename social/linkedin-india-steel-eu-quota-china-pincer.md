# LinkedIn draft — India pincer (india-steel-eu-quota-china-pincer)

Europe just cut the door to 18.3 Mt with 50% out-of-quota duties. Indian mills expect EU exports down 40%.

Plan B — pivot home — has a problem: Chinese steel is landing $52–63/t below domestic grades. Volume comes home; margin doesn't.

A mill can survive losing an export market. It struggles to survive losing the export market and the home price at the same time. The real fix isn't another duty — it's the cost base.

# LinkedIn draft — Playbook (cbam-playbook-steel-exporters)

CBAM is the first trade barrier that doesn't care if your steel is cheaper, faster, or better. It prices something most exporters have never measured.

The playbook that keeps Europe, in six moves — and the order IS the strategy: Measure (granular, full value chain — not annual averages). Prove (accredited verification; the queue is real). Cut (plant ops: efficiency, energy substitution, raw materials — no hydrogen plant required). Cascade (your suppliers' emissions are your price now). Systematize (spreadsheets won't survive an audit). Hedge (credits are an instrument, not a strategy).

Run them out of order and each undermines the next. Run them in order and each compounds.

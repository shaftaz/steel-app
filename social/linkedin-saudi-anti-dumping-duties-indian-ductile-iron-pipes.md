# LinkedIn draft — Gulf wall (saudi-anti-dumping-duties-indian-ductile-iron-pipes)

Trade defense used to be a rich-country instrument. Saudi Arabia just made it everyone's.

Five-year anti-dumping duties on Indian ductile iron pipes: 16.96% for Electrosteel, 29.94% for everyone else. After similar duties on Chinese and Taiwanese pipes, that's not a dispute — it's a policy.

Two lessons: (1) the Gulf is no longer a wall-free diversification market, exactly when the EU quota squeeze needed it to be; (2) the 13-point gap between the cooperating exporter's rate and the residual is the price of showing up to your own trial.

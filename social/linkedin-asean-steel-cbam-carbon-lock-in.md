# LinkedIn draft — ASEAN three clocks (asean-steel-cbam-carbon-lock-in)

ASEAN tripled its steel output — 14 Mt to 49 Mt in a decade. Impressive. Now the bill is arriving.

New Ramboll/SGBC analysis: regional emissions run 1.78–2.00 tCO₂/t (well above EU benchmarks), 68.7 Mt of NEW coal-based BF-BOF capacity is in the pipeline — much of it Chinese state-linked financed — and 75% of the cleaner EAF fleet is over 20 years old.

Three clocks are running: assets (25–30 yr lock-in), regulation (CBAM's ramp), and verification (MRV). Only one resets cheaply — and here's the inversion most miss: without MRV, producers pay CBAM's punitive default values regardless of actual performance. You can lose Europe not because your steel is dirty, but because you can't prove it isn't.

Measurement capability is now market access.

# LinkedIn draft — Market re-read (seamless-steel-pipe-market-trends-2026)

Still planning pipe demand on the 2023 regional map? Three buoys have moved.

The market: ~$139bn in 2026 → ~$210bn by 2033, seamless holding 66%+, APAC over 60%. That held. What changed: CCUS pipe went from "trend" to codified standard (API 5L 47th ed). The US became a price-walled premium market pulling capacity inward. And the Gulf — the eternally open import market — started imposing anti-dumping duties of its own.

Bigger, more specified, more alloyed — and far more politically shaped than the old decks assumed.

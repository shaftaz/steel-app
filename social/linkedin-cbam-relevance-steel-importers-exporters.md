# LinkedIn draft — Six seats (cbam-relevance-steel-importers-exporters)

CBAM is legally an importer's obligation. Commercially, it's everyone's price.

Six seats now need the same calculation: the EU importer (budget + supplier arbitration), the non-EU mill (your offer is carbon-inclusive whether you price it or not), the trader (quoting 2027 DDP on 2026 factors is the textbook error), EU procurement (landed costs diverging by carbon profile), finance (it's a curve to 2034, not a number), and analysts (CBAM re-ranks exporting origins by emissions intensity).

The seat that runs the number first holds the edge over the seats that don't.

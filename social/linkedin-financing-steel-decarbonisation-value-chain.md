# LinkedIn draft — Green steel finance (financing-steel-decarbonisation-value-chain)

The scarcest input to green steel is no longer technology. It's a customer.

The Climate Bonds Initiative's new report says it directly: demand — not tech — is the bottleneck. And financing plants alone won't fix it. The investable unit is the whole chain: high-grade ore, renewable power, hydrogen, scrap systems, and committed buyers.

The winners will assemble the full stack: credible transition plan + innovative finance + secured inputs + long-dated offtake. Everyone else will watch their cost of capital drift up.

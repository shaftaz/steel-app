# LinkedIn draft — Two escapes (japan-korea-steel-overseas-expansion-green-hydrogen)

While everyone watched the walls go up, Japan and Korea quietly published the escape plan.

POSCO: overseas production doubling to 10 MTPA by 2031 — Indonesia, India, US. Nippon Steel: US Steel investment accelerating, forecast raised to ¥11.2tn. JFE: Japan's first green-hydrogen DRI.

Two escapes, one doctrine: build inside the walls (a tariff taxes a cargo but rewards the factory behind it), and build what the walls can't tax (verified low-carbon steel, as carbon border pricing spreads).

Trade walls don't stop competition. They relocate it — from cargoes to capital.

# LinkedIn draft — The state or nobody (europe-steel-state-ownership-bankability-gap)

Two of Europe's biggest steel assets, two outcomes. British Steel: nationalised in July 2026. Former Ilva: one bidder says it asked 30 banks — none would lend.

Europe's steel problem isn't technology. Both Ilva bidders planned EAFs. It's bankability: expensive energy, flat demand with no reliable buyer for green premiums, and doubt that carbon policy will last the life of the asset.

More grants won't fix that. Contracted demand, contracted energy, and durable carbon value might.

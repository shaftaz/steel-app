# LinkedIn draft — India pillar (indian-steel-industry-challenges)

India grew steel capacity at 9.5% a year for four years. Energy per tonne? Flat. CO₂ per tonne? Flat.

That contrast is the whole story of the 2030 target. Volume is not the constraint — three gates are: Inputs (11% high-grade ore, 85–90% imported coking coal, scarce scrap). Structure (coal-DRI reliance, road logistics, R&D at 0.2–0.3% of revenue vs 1–3% for Japan/Korea/EU). Markets (electrical steel still imported, export walls rising).

You cannot import 90% of your coking coal and export cost leadership.

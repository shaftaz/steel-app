# LinkedIn draft — POSCO's three answers (posco-three-answers-steel-strategy)

POSCO is building a hydrogen demo plant in Korea, a DRI-EAF mill in Louisiana, and a blast furnace in India — at the same time.

Contradiction? No. A portfolio. Earn (India BF: growth market, bankable route). Defend (US EAF: inside the tariff wall). Invent (Korea hydrogen: the route it'll need at home).

The open bet: can a 2031 blast furnace earn enough, for long enough, to fund Korea's transition before its own carbon costs rise?

Every steelmaker will need all three legs. The question is who can afford them.

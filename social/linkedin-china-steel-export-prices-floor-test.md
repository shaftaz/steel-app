# LinkedIn draft — Floor test (china-steel-export-prices-floor-test)

Update on the China export price sequence we've tracked since July: rally without buyers → rollover → and now, the floor test.

This week's tape: HRC steady at $480–483 FOB, and for the first time this down-leg, real enquiries — from deep-sea markets, drawn by how cheap Chinese steel has become. Billet exporters say there's little room left to cut.

The distinction that matters: stabilization is sellers unable to cut; recovery is buyers choosing to transact. Enquiries are questions, not commitments — conversion over the next fortnight settles it.

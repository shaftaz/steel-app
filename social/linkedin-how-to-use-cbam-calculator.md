# LinkedIn draft — CBAM how-to (how-to-use-cbam-calculator)

CBAM went definitive in 2026. Most of the steel trade still hasn't run its number.

Here's what 10,000 t of HRC from India costs this year: about €6.1/tonne, 805 certificates. Sounds manageable — because the 2.5% phase-in is doing the softening. The same cargo's cost climbs every year to 2034 as the factor heads to 100%.

Two mistakes we keep seeing: forward contracts priced on this year's factor, and importers paying default values without ever asking suppliers for verified emissions data. Both are checkable in two minutes.

We built a free CBAM calculator — EU & UK regimes, all covered products, defaults vs verified, cost curve to 2034. Link in comments. Know your number before your counterparty knows theirs. #CBAM #steel #trade

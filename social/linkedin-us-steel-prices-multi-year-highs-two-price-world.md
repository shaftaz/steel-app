# LinkedIn draft — Two-price world (us-steel-prices-multi-year-highs-two-price-world)

US hot-rolled: $1,180/short ton — highest since June 2022. Chinese export HRC: ~$480/tonne FOB. Same commodity. Two and a half times the price.

That's not freight. That's the wall premium — the measurable amount protection lets a domestic price detach from the world price. Lead times of 5–12 weeks say the tightness is real; a 12-week queue can't be faked.

The catch: the wall protects steel, but it can't protect everything made of steel. US manufacturers now carry input costs their overseas competitors don't — and the spread is summoning supply, including foreign-owned mills being built inside the wall to capture it.

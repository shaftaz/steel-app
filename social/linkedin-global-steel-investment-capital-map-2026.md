# LinkedIn draft — The capital map (global-steel-investment-capital-map-2026)

Add up the biggest cross-border steel commitments of 2025–26: roughly $42bn.

US: ~$31.7bn (Nippon–US Steel, Hyundai–POSCO Louisiana). India: ~$6.2bn (AMNS India, POSCO–JSW Odisha). MENA: ~$4.5bn (Baowu–Aramco–PIF Ras Al Khair, Tosyali Algeria). Europe: zero.

Steel capital follows three magnets — protection, growth, cheap energy. Europe has the strictest carbon policy in the world and none of the three.

The most revealing line came from POSCO: profits from global markets will help fund decarbonisation back in Korea. Carbon isn't attracting capital. It's being financed from somewhere else.

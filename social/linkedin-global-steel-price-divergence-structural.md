# LinkedIn draft — Three-price world (global-steel-price-divergence-structural)

Update on the two-price world: it grew a third tier, and the market stopped calling it temporary.

US HRC: ~$1,196/short ton on CRU, futures ~$1,213 (~$1,320/t metric). Europe: >$810/t. Asia: ~$480/t FOB. Same commodity — a ~$840/t ladder, built of tariffs, quotas, and carbon borders, not freight.

When a spread becomes structural: benchmarks decouple (there is no "world steel price" anymore), arbitrage migrates from steel to steel-content (finished goods cross walls that tonnes can't), and capacity follows price instead of cost — which is why Asian producers are building inside the walls.

Every model that assumes convergence is now quietly wrong. Full analysis — SteelMath blog, link in comments. #steel #trade #prices

# LinkedIn draft — X-grade dilemma (api-5l-pipeline-steel-grade-selection)

Upgrading a pipeline from X52 to X70 cuts wall thickness ~25%. On a 500 km line, that's ~30,000 tonnes of steel you never buy.

So why do deepwater projects deliberately buy WEAKER steel? Because offshore design is governed by collapse resistance and lay-barge economics — at $300–500k/day, the most expensive steel is the steel that slows the barge. Heavy-wall X65 beats X80 where it matters.

The grade decision is really three ledgers: tonnage, weldability (CE_IIW ≤ 0.43%, Pcm ≤ 0.25% — specify it numerically), and lifetime. Optimize one, audit all three.
